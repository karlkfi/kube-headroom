# Fix tenants.html scrolling sideways on phones

The landing page `website/landing/tenants.html` is 443px wide at both a 320px and a 390px viewport, so it scrolls sideways on a phone. Measured 2026-11-05 by loading each page of the built site (`make site-landing` plus the VitePress build) at several viewport widths and comparing `documentElement.scrollWidth` to the viewport. `index.html`, `faq.html` and `404.html` fit at both widths.

The widest element is the "See why your ceiling is what it is" item under "The practical bits" (`.howto-item`, right edge at 443px). Its `<pre>` holds a long unbroken `kubectl get pod … -o jsonpath` line, and the suspect mechanism is that, as a grid item with the default `min-width: auto`, it stops the single-column `.howto` grid from shrinking below that line. Not confirmed, neither that mechanism nor that `min-width: 0` on `.howto-item` plus `overflow-x: auto` on the `<pre>` clears it. The hero `svg.day-art` also extends past the right edge (to 360px at a 320px viewport) and may be clipped by the hero rather than widening the page, so check it after the first fix.

WCAG 1.4.10 sets 320px as the width content must reflow to. Do not hide it with `overflow-x: hidden` on the page, which clips the text instead of fixing the layout.
